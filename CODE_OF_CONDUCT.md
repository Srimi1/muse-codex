# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of
technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting responsibility for impact.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and project security boundaries.
- Help make technical discussion accessible to newcomers.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully, including decisions to close or
  defer work that falls outside the project's scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained
disruption, deliberate exposure of secrets or private data, and personal
attacks are not tolerated. Publishing proprietary Muse artifacts or another
person's prompts, credentials, or account information is also prohibited.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
comments, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a project-specific concern, contact the maintainer through
[@Srimi1's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Srimi1). Do not publish sensitive
details in an issue. Conduct that violates GitHub policy can be reported through
[GitHub's private abuse-reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict,
or permanently ban participation based on the context and severity of a
violation. Reports will be handled as privately as the available channel allows.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant and GitHub Community
Guidelines.
